One app. More ways to see your game.

From a practice swing to a shared scorecard, keep your golf moments together.

01 / CREATE

Add Ball Trail

Record or import a video, trim your shot, and refine the suggested path. Choose a trail style and export a shareable MP4.

02 / COMPARE

Compare Swings

Play two clips together, align their impact moments, slow things down, and step through your swing frame by frame.

03 / MEASURE

Measure Distance

Set a target on the map and see the distance in meters or yards, with terrain elevation and club suggestions when available.

04 / PLAY

Keep Score

Start a 9- or 18-hole round for up to four players. Save scores as you play and share the finished round summary.

05 / LEARN

Your Shot Stats

Save club and carry details, filter your shot history, and explore your average and longest recorded distances.

06 / KEEP

My Videos

Keep your saved shots on your device. Revisit a clip, adjust its tracer, or share the exported video with friends.
