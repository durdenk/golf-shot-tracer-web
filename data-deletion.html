LEGAL / DATA DELETION

Delete your data

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This page explains how to delete data related to Golf Swing & Shot Tracer (Android package com.cukkacreatives.golf.shot.swing.trace), provided by Cukka Creatives ("we" or "us").

There is no account to delete. The app does not use accounts, and your videos are processed on your device. A shot video reaches our server only if you chose to send it to help improve tracking, and you can delete those from the app at any time. Your shots, scorecards and settings live on your phone, so you can delete them yourself. You can ask us to delete anything you sent us by email.

1. Delete the data on your phone

Saved shot projects, source and exported videos, recordings made in the app, shot details (names, clubs, carry values), scorecards, player names, your local profile name, preferences and usage counters are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

  • All saved shots: open Settings → Delete All Shots, or My Videos → Delete All. This removes saved shot projects, their source and exported videos, and recordings made in the app. It does not remove scorecards or preferences.
  • Everything in the app: open Android Settings → Apps → Golf Swing & Shot Tracer → Storage and tap Clear storage (or Clear data), or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device. This removes all local projects, recordings, scores and preferences.

Deleting shots on your phone does not delete shot videos you already sent to us; see section 2. Deletion cannot be undone. Videos you saved to your gallery, or exported or shared to other apps, are outside the app and are not removed; delete them where you saved or sent them.

Android backup and device transfer are enabled for the app. Depending on your device and settings, a copy of eligible app data may exist in your device backup (for example, your Google account backup). Manage or delete those copies in your device's backup settings.

2. Ask us to delete your data

The data we hold directly is email correspondence you send us, notes you send with Settings → Send feedback, and shot videos you chose to send to help improve tracking. Your scores and unsent shots never reach us.

Shot videos you sent

  • In the app: open Settings → Delete my sent videos. This deletes all videos and data sent with your contributor identifier from our server.
  • By email: include your contributor identifier, shown in Settings. If you no longer have it (for example after reinstalling the app without a backup), include your device model and the approximate dates you sent videos, and we will find and delete matching videos.
  • Stop sending: turn off Settings → Send my videos to improve tracking. Shots that have not finished uploading are cancelled.

Email and in-app feedback

  1. Email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject "Golf Swing & Shot Tracer Data Deletion Request".
  2. Send it from the email address you used to contact us, or include that address and enough detail to identify the correspondence. For in-app feedback, include the approximate date and the text of your note.
  3. Do not send passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information.

We reply to confirm your request and delete the data within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Support emails you sent us, with any attachments, including your deletion request once it is handled.
  • In-app feedback notes you sent from Settings.
  • Sent shot videos, including their audio, tracer and analysis data, and device and app information. Without a deletion request, they are deleted at the latest 24 months after we received them.
  • Analytics data processed by Google Analytics for Firebase on our behalf. It is tied to a random app-instance identifier that we cannot link to you by name or email. Where we can identify your data, we delete it; otherwise it expires under Google Analytics data retention settings. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling resets this identifier.
  • Crash reports processed by Firebase Crashlytics on our behalf. They are tied to random installation identifiers that we cannot link to you by name or email, and Google keeps them for 90 days and then removes them (see Privacy and Security in Firebase). Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling resets these identifiers.

4. What is kept

  • Information needed to meet legal obligations or prevent abuse may be kept as long as that need lasts.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own retention practices and tax, accounting and fraud-prevention laws. We do not store them on a server of our own.

5. Data held by other services

Some features use external services that handle data under their own policies. Deleting app data does not delete information they hold independently.

  • Google Maps and Google Play services (distance screen): manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.
  • Open Topo Data (terrain elevation): receives coordinates and connection information; we do not control its server logs. See opentopodata.org.
  • Premium subscription: deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel it. Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Google Play reviews: edit or delete your review on the app's page in Google Play.
