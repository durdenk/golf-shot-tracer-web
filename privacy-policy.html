Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how Golf Swing & Shot Tracer (Android package com.cukkacreatives.golf.shot.swing.trace), provided by Cukka Creatives ("we" or "us"), handles information.

No account is required. Videos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Map, terrain, and usage analytics features connect to external services, as explained below.

1. Information Used by the App

  • Videos and audio: Clips you select or record, their audio tracks, and generated thumbnails are used for playback, shot analysis, editing, comparison, and export.
  • Saved golf data: Shot names, tracer settings, club selections, carry values, round names, player names, hole scores, and statistics are stored locally.
  • Preferences: Your local profile name, onboarding state, distance units, and other app settings are saved on your device. A profile name does not create an online account.
  • Location: With your permission, the distance screen obtains your device location to measure distance to a selected target.
  • Usage analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase processes app usage, screen views, operation outcomes and timings, and device/app information. See section 5 for details.
  • Support messages: If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send.

2. Video Processing and Permissions

Shot analysis, tracer editing, swing comparison, and video export run on your device. Imported source clips are copied into the app's private storage so saved projects can be edited later. We do not receive these clips through the app's processing features.

Camera access is used to record a video. Microphone access is optional and is used to include sound in a recording. Video selection uses the Android system picker rather than unrestricted access to your media library. You may deny or revoke permissions in Android settings; the related feature may then be unavailable.

3. Maps, Location, and Terrain Elevation

The distance screen uses Google Maps and Google Play services. These services may process location, device, connection, and service-use information under Google's Privacy Policy. Location is requested for this feature; the app does not request background location permission.

When a location and target are selected, the terrain feature sends both latitude/longitude pairs over HTTPS to the Open Topo Data API to obtain approximate ground elevation. The service also receives connection information such as your IP address. Our app keeps a temporary elevation cache in memory. We do not control the provider's server logs or retention practices.

You can use video and scorekeeping features without location permission. You can revoke location access or avoid the distance screen to stop location-based requests from that feature.

4. Local Storage, Sharing, and Android Backup

Saved source videos, exported clips, project details, scores, and preferences remain in app storage until removed. If you share a video or round summary, the receiving app or recipient obtains the content you choose to share and handles it under its own terms.

Android backup and device transfer are enabled in the app configuration. Depending on your device, system settings, platform limits, and backup provider, eligible app data may be backed up or transferred. Manage those copies using your device's backup controls. This system-managed behavior is separate from our on-device video processing.

5. Advertising, Purchases, and Analytics

The app uses Google Play Billing to retrieve localized prices, purchase Premium subscriptions, check subscription access, restore purchases, and confirm completed purchases. Google Play processes payments using your Google account and handles payment details under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase tokens to provide Premium access; these are held in memory and are not sent to our servers or included in our custom analytics events. If prices cannot be retrieved, estimated USD prices are shown and checkout is unavailable. The app does not integrate an advertising SDK.

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand feature usage and improve the app. Its SDK processes app-instance identifiers, app and device information, usage events, and approximate location derived from IP addresses, and may automatically record purchase activity. Our custom events record screen names, video loading, shot analysis, export and save outcomes, operation and clip durations, and analysis summaries such as detection counts and confidence. We do not include videos, audio, thumbnails, file paths, profile or player names, shot titles, or precise location coordinates in these events.

Advertising ID collection and personalized advertising signals are disabled in the app configuration. Google handles analytics data under Google's Privacy Policy; see also Google Analytics data collection information. Removing local app data does not automatically remove analytics data already processed by Google.

6. Support, Retention, and Security

Support correspondence may be retained as needed to respond, investigate a report, and meet applicable obligations. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose support information where required by law. Local app data relies on Android's app storage protections; external map and elevation requests use encrypted connections. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

7. Data Deletion

In the app, Settings → Delete All Shots or My Videos → Delete All removes saved shot projects, their source and exported videos, and recordings made in the app. This does not delete scorecards, preferences, videos in your device gallery, or copies you exported outside the app.

There is no app account to delete. To remove all data from the installed app, open Android Settings → Apps → Golf Swing & Shot Tracer → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device. This removes local projects, recordings, scores, and preferences.

Copies exported or shared outside the app and system backups are separate. Delete those copies in the relevant receiving app, storage location, or backup service. Removing local app data does not delete information held independently by Google or Open Topo Data.

Deleting shots, clearing storage, or uninstalling does not cancel a Premium subscription. Manage subscriptions and cancellation in Google Play. Purchase records held by Google are subject to Google's retention practices.

To request deletion of support correspondence held by us, email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject "Golf Swing & Shot Tracer Data Deletion Request". Include the email address used to contact us and enough detail to identify the correspondence. We will handle requests subject to applicable retention requirements.

8. This Website

These static pages do not include our own analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or forms. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information under its Privacy Statement. Following a Google Play or other external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You can control permissions, edit local profile information, clear app storage, manage system backups, and choose what to share. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us about information we hold; contact the relevant provider about information it independently holds. Providers may process information in countries other than your own.

10. Children, Changes, and Contact

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support correspondence. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it. We may update this policy as the app changes; the date above identifies the latest revision.
