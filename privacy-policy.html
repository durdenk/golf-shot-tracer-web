Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how Golf Swing & Shot Tracer (Android package com.cukkacreatives.golf.shot.swing.trace), provided by Cukka Creatives ("we" or "us"), handles information.

No account is required. Videos are processed on your device. A shot video is uploaded to our server only if you choose to send it to help us improve ball tracking; this is off by default (see section 3). Map, terrain, usage analytics, and crash reporting features connect to external services, as explained below.

1. Information Used by the App

  • Videos and audio: Clips you select or record, their audio tracks, and generated thumbnails are used for playback, shot analysis, editing, comparison, and export.
  • Saved golf data: Shot names, tracer settings, club selections, carry values, round names, player names, hole scores, and statistics are stored locally.
  • Preferences: Your local profile name, onboarding state, distance units, and other app settings are saved on your device. A profile name does not create an online account.
  • Location: With your permission, the distance screen obtains your device location to measure distance to a selected target.
  • Usage analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase processes app usage, screen views, operation outcomes and timings, and device/app information. See section 6 for details.
  • Crash reports: If the app crashes or stops responding, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report with technical diagnostics and random installation identifiers. See section 6 for details.
  • Support messages: If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send.
  • In-app feedback: If you use Settings → Send feedback, we receive the note you type, the app name and version, and a random identifier generated by the app on your device (not your Advertising ID or a hardware identifier). The identifier is used only to limit how many notes can be sent per hour. Feedback is sent over an encrypted connection; please do not include personal information in your note.
  • Shot videos you choose to send: If you send a shot to help improve tracking, we receive that shot's videos with audio, its tracer and analysis data, basic device and app information, and a random contributor identifier. See section 3 for details.

2. Video Processing and Permissions

Shot analysis, tracer editing, swing comparison, and video export run on your device. Imported source clips are copied into the app's private storage so saved projects can be edited later. We do not receive these clips through the app's processing features; we receive a shot's videos only when you choose to send them as described in section 3.

Camera access is used to record a video. Microphone access is optional and is used to include sound in a recording. Video selection uses the Android system picker rather than unrestricted access to your media library. You may deny or revoke permissions in Android settings; the related feature may then be unavailable.

3. Sending Shot Videos to Improve Tracking

This feature is optional and off by default. Nothing is sent unless you choose one of these:

  • Send a single shot: on the result screen, tap Result wrong? Send it to us and confirm with Send on the consent screen. You are asked for every shot you send this way.
  • Send shots automatically: turn on Settings → Send my videos to improve tracking and confirm on the consent screen. While it is on, each shot you process is sent in the background without asking again. You can turn it off at any time.

What we receive for each sent shot:

  • The trimmed shot clip, and the part of the original video from up to 10 seconds before to 10 seconds after the trimmed part (at most 60 seconds). The rest of the original video is not sent.
  • The audio of these videos. We use sound to detect the moment of impact. Speech or other people's voices may be included if they were recorded.
  • The trim times, the ball path found by the app, any corrections you made to it, the calculated results (such as distance and apex), and tracer style settings.
  • App version, device model, and the videos' resolution and frame rate.
  • A random contributor identifier created by the app (not your Advertising ID, a hardware identifier, or an account), and the version of the consent text you agreed to.
  • As with any internet connection, our server receives your IP address. It is used only for security and rate limiting and is not stored with your videos.

Location metadata is removed from the videos before sending. We do not receive your name, email address, or account details.

How we use it: only to review tracking errors and improve ball tracking and impact detection, including training and testing machine-learning models. We do not use sent videos for advertising or marketing, do not publish them, and do not sell or share them with third parties, except the hosting provider that runs our server on our behalf. We process this data on the basis of your consent.

How it is sent and stored: videos are uploaded over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, only while your device is on Wi-Fi. They are stored on our own server with restricted access, and deleted at the latest 24 months after they were received.

Free plan bonus: on the free plan, sending a shot gives you one extra new shot for that day, at most once per day. Declining has no effect on your use of the app.

Other people and children: only send videos you have the right to share. If other people can be seen or heard in a video, send it only with their permission. If a child appears in a video, it should be sent only with the agreement of their parent or guardian.

Withdrawing consent: turn off the setting at any time; shots that have not finished uploading are then cancelled. Deleting a shot before its upload finishes also cancels the upload. To delete videos you have already sent, see section 8. Your contributor identifier is shown in Settings and is included in Android backup, so it can return if you restore the app from a backup.

4. Maps, Location, and Terrain Elevation

The distance screen uses Google Maps and Google Play services. These services may process location, device, connection, and service-use information under Google's Privacy Policy. Location is requested for this feature; the app does not request background location permission.

When a location and target are selected, the terrain feature sends both latitude/longitude pairs over HTTPS to the Open Topo Data API to obtain approximate ground elevation. The service also receives connection information such as your IP address. Our app keeps a temporary elevation cache in memory. We do not control the provider's server logs or retention practices.

You can use video and scorekeeping features without location permission. You can revoke location access or avoid the distance screen to stop location-based requests from that feature.

5. Local Storage, Sharing, and Android Backup

Saved source videos, exported clips, project details, scores, and preferences remain in app storage until removed. If you share a video or round summary, the receiving app or recipient obtains the content you choose to share and handles it under its own terms.

Android backup and device transfer are enabled in the app configuration. Depending on your device, system settings, platform limits, and backup provider, eligible app data may be backed up or transferred. Manage those copies using your device's backup controls. This system-managed behavior is separate from our on-device video processing.

6. Advertising, Purchases, Analytics, and Crash Reporting

The app uses Google Play Billing to retrieve localized prices, purchase Premium subscriptions, check subscription access, restore purchases, and confirm completed purchases. Google Play processes payments using your Google account and handles payment details under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase tokens to provide Premium access; these are held in memory and are not sent to our servers or included in our custom analytics events. If prices cannot be retrieved, estimated USD prices are shown and checkout is unavailable. The app does not integrate an advertising SDK.

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand feature usage and improve the app. Its SDK processes app-instance identifiers, app and device information, usage events, and approximate location derived from IP addresses, and may automatically record purchase activity. Our custom events record screen names, video loading, shot analysis, export and save outcomes, operation and clip durations, and analysis summaries such as detection counts and confidence. We do not include videos, audio, thumbnails, file paths, profile or player names, shot titles, or precise location coordinates in these events.

Google Analytics for Firebase may also collect your device's Android Advertising ID, which it uses for analytics and measurement. Personalized advertising signals are disabled in the app configuration, and the app does not currently show ads. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Android settings (for example, Settings → Privacy → Ads; menu names vary by device). Google handles analytics data under Google's Privacy Policy; see also Google Analytics data collection information. Removing local app data does not automatically remove analytics data already processed by Google.

We use Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes. When the app crashes or stops responding, Crashlytics sends a report containing the error details and stack trace, the app version, device model and manufacturer, Android version, device state at the time of the crash (such as orientation, free memory, free storage, and whether the device is rooted), and random installation and session identifiers generated by Firebase. Crash reports do not include your videos, audio, thumbnails, profile or player names, shot titles, or location coordinates, and they do not use your Advertising ID. Crash reports are collected only in the published version of the app. Google keeps crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days and then removes them; see Privacy and Security in Firebase. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling resets these identifiers.

7. Support, Retention, and Security

Support correspondence may be retained as needed to respond, investigate a report, and meet applicable obligations. Shot videos you send are kept for at most 24 months, or until you delete them. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose support information where required by law. Local app data relies on Android's app storage protections; external map and elevation requests, analytics, crash reports, in-app feedback, and shot video uploads use encrypted connections. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

8. Data Deletion

In the app, Settings → Delete All Shots or My Videos → Delete All removes saved shot projects, their source and exported videos, and recordings made in the app. This does not delete scorecards, preferences, videos in your device gallery, copies you exported outside the app, or shot videos you already sent to us.

To delete shot videos you sent to us, open Settings → Delete my sent videos. This deletes all videos and data sent with your contributor identifier from our server. You can also email us with your contributor identifier (shown in Settings). If you no longer have it, for example after reinstalling the app without a backup, include your device model and the approximate dates you sent videos, and we will find and delete matching videos.

There is no app account to delete. To remove all data from the installed app, open Android Settings → Apps → Golf Swing & Shot Tracer → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device. This removes local projects, recordings, scores, and preferences.

Copies exported or shared outside the app and system backups are separate. Delete those copies in the relevant receiving app, storage location, or backup service. Removing local app data does not delete information held independently by Google or Open Topo Data.

Deleting shots, clearing storage, or uninstalling does not cancel a Premium subscription. Manage subscriptions and cancellation in Google Play. Purchase records held by Google are subject to Google's retention practices.

To request deletion of support correspondence held by us, email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject "Golf Swing & Shot Tracer Data Deletion Request". Include the email address used to contact us and enough detail to identify the correspondence. For in-app feedback, include the approximate date and the text of your note. For sent shot videos, include the details described above. We will handle requests subject to applicable retention requirements.

9. This Website

These static pages do not include our own analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or forms. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information under its Privacy Statement. Following a Google Play or other external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You can control permissions, edit local profile information, clear app storage, manage system backups, and choose what to share. You can withdraw your consent to sending shot videos at any time; this does not affect videos sent before you withdrew it, which you can delete as described in section 8. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us about information we hold; contact the relevant provider about information it independently holds. Providers may process information in countries other than your own.

11. Children, Changes, and Contact

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support correspondence, in-app feedback, or sent shot videos. A video in which a child appears should be sent only with the agreement of their parent or guardian. If you believe a child's personal information has been sent to us, contact us so we can delete it. We may update this policy as the app changes; the date above identifies the latest revision.
